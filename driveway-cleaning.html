Why Clean Your Driveway?

Driveways take a beating — oil, grime, algae and tyre marks build up faster than you'd expect. A proper clean makes a big difference.

01

Boost Kerb Appeal

A clean driveway is one of the first things people notice. It instantly lifts the look of your whole property.

02

Remove Slip Hazards

Algae and wet grime make driveways dangerously slippery. Regular cleaning keeps it safe for your family and visitors.

03

Protect the Surface

Built-up grime and organic matter can degrade concrete and pavers over time. Cleaning extends the life of your driveway.

How long does a driveway clean take?
Your driveway will be clean the same day. We work efficiently to make sure everything is done in a single visit.
Do I need to be home?
Not always. As long as we have access to the driveway and an outdoor water tap, we can usually carry out the work while you're out.
Is it safe for all driveway types?
Yes. We adjust the pressure and technique based on the surface — concrete, pavers, brick, exposed aggregate and more.
Will it remove oil stains?
We can significantly reduce oil staining. Deep-set stains may not fully disappear but will be noticeably improved.

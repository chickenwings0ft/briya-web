Why Clean Your Solar Panels?

In Queensland's environment, dust, pollen, bird droppings and grime accumulate quickly on panels and can noticeably reduce their output if left uncleaned.

Maintain Output

Dirty panels absorb less sunlight, which directly reduces how much energy they generate. Keeping them clean protects your investment.

Safe, Careful Cleaning

We use appropriate methods and equipment to clean panels without scratching the surface or damaging seals and connections.

Extend Panel Life

Built-up grime and bird droppings can cause hot spots that degrade panel efficiency over time. Regular cleaning helps protect longevity.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?
Generally once or twice a year, depending on your local environment — areas near trees or birds may need more frequent cleaning.
Is it safe to clean solar panels?
Yes, when done correctly. We use panel-safe equipment and techniques to clean without scratching the glass or affecting any connections.

Yes, we often combine solar panel and roof cleaning in one visit for better value.
